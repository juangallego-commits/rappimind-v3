## Company & Product Context

**Rappi** is a Latin American super-app founded in Bogotá (2015), operating in 9+ countries. It connects users with restaurants, grocery, pharmacy, retail, and financial services via on-demand delivery.

**Rappi's mission:** *Liberar a las personas de la fricción cotidiana* — free people from daily friction by connecting them with what they need, when they need it.

### Product Architecture

Rappi operates a **master-brand + endorsed sub-brands** model:

| Brand | Description | Visual System |
|---|---|---|
| **Rappi** | Master platform | Neon gradient (external), app secondary palette (in-app) |
| **Rappi Turbo** | Ultra-fast grocery delivery (10–15 min) | Verde Turbo in-app; Neon outside |
| **Rappi Turbo Restaurantes** | Ultra-fast restaurant delivery | Same as Turbo |
| **Rappi Pro** | Subscription membership (light) | #ECEFF3 bg + gold accent |
| **Pro Black** | Premium membership (dark) | #2A2B2B bg + gold accent |
| **Rappi Pay / RappiCard** | Fintech | Guidelines in construction |
| **Rappi Travel** | Travel services | No separate visual identity |

**RappiAds** is Rappi's advertising product — enabling brands to buy paid media (Meta, TikTok, UAC, programmatic, display) and own-media placements (in-app banners, push, SMS, WhatsApp) across the Rappi ecosystem.

## CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS

### Brand Voice: El Amigo

Rappi speaks like a friend who has already tried what they're recommending — direct, without exaggeration. The archetype is a cosmopolitan, bilingual NSE A/B speaker from CDMX: has taste, has criteria, speaks at eye level.

**Core tonal axes:**
- **Direct, not blunt** — benefit in the first 7 words, no preamble
- **Warm, not effusive** — closeness through precision, not adjectives
- **Urgent, not pressuring** — inform temporality, don't manipulate
- **Empathy that resolves** — max 2 recognition lines, then solve it

### Register & Vocabulary

**Active vocabulary:** ya · nomás · aquí · hoy · sin · listo · top · deal · craving · mood · tranqui · anyway · BTW · de verdad · que aproveche

**Prohibited words:** cuate · carnal · órale · wey · estimado/a · lamentamos · revolucionario · potencializar · usted · bestie · amix · !! (double exclamation)

### Copy Rules

- **Tuteo always** — never "usted" or "su"
- **Benefit first** — in the first 7 words of every piece
- **Present active voice** — "Tu pedido llega", not "Tu pedido fue entregado"
- **Max 1 emoji** — only at the start, only in push CRM with brief. Zero in OOH, UX, or email
- **CTAs = max 3 words** — "Prueba Turbo", "Aprovecha hoy", "Usa tus créditos"
- **No double punctuation** — never `!!` or `??`; `¡!` max 1 per piece for genuine celebration

### Tone by Situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Celebration / delivery | Warm, direct | "Tu pedido llegó. Que aproveche." |
| Error / apology | Honest, practical | "Tardó más de lo normal. Ya lo sé, va en camino." |
| Offer urgency | Informative, not pressuring | "Termina hoy. Ya sabes." |
| Onboarding | Clear, confident | "Así funciona. Fácil. Sin drama." |
| Surprise / benefit | Contained joy | "Esto es para ti. Ya está aplicado." |

### Country Signatures

| Country | Signature |
|---|---|
| 🇲🇽 México | Pídelo por Rappi |
| 🇨🇴 🇵🇪 🇪🇨 | Pide un Rappi |
| 🇦🇷 🇺🇾 | Pedí un Rappi |
| 🇨🇱 | Pídelo por Rappi |

### Grammar Essentials

- Percentages: `30% OFF` (no space before %)
- Prices: no decimals in campaign copy — `$99` not `$99.00`
- Time: `10 min` (space between number and abbreviation)
- Thousands separators: period in CO/AR (`$15.000`), comma in EC (`$15,000`)

## VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

### Color System

**Four non-interchangeable color layers:**

1. **Neon (principal)** — All external communication, paid media, OOH, printed. Always applied as a gradient (`#FF7A4D → #FF2526 → #FF4583`), angle −45° base. Never as flat individual colors.
2. **App secondary palette** — In-app only (own media). Celeste `#D9F1FF`, Morado `#E5DCFF`, Rosado `#F4DFF6`, Lavanda `#F4F0FC`, Beige `#FFF0DD`. One secondary + Neon = valid. Two secondaries without Neon = not allowed.
3. **Turbo verde** — Exclusive to Turbo in-app pieces. `#248F63` (light), `#00453E` (dark). Never in external media.
4. **Pro / Pro Black gold** — `#F0B769` / `#E8A24C`. Exclusive to Pro and Pro Black contexts. Never in general Rappi pieces.

**Text on backgrounds:**
- Neon/gradient → white only
- Turbo verde → white only
- Pro light (`#ECEFF3`) → black (`#000000`)
- Pro Black (`#2A2B2B`) → white (`#FFFFFF`)
- App pastels → black (`#000000`)

### Typography

**PP Object Sans** is the official typeface for all digital and printed campaign materials. Four weights:
- **Regular (400)** — legal texts, terms & conditions
- **Medium (500)** — body copy, descriptions, subtitles
- **Bold (700)** — supporting headlines
- **Heavy (900)** — main headlines, deals, prices, benefits

Letter spacing: tight on display (`−0.045em`); normal on body. No mixing with other typefaces in the same piece.

**Email only:** Jornada Sans (not available as a webfont in this DS; use PP Object Sans as fallback in digital prototypes).

### Photography

Two systems:
- **Studio on Neon background** — product on the neon gradient; commercial, premium, slight depth of field; 3/4 angle from above; no full overhead. For campaign banners and high-hierarchy pieces.
- **Lifestyle** — matte neon-orange textured surface; natural diffuse light; hands only (no arms, no faces, no full bodies); ceramic tableware; slight background blur. Canon EOS R5 / 50mm reference.

**Never:** dark/dramatic lighting, cyberpunk purples/blues, AI-generated imagery, top-down flat angle, fine-dining aesthetics.

### Backgrounds

- Neon gradient is the main campaign background (external).
- White is the secondary background for catalogue and email.
- App secondary pastels for in-app pieces.
- Photography as full-bleed on some formats, always with safe area for text.

### Motion & Animation

- Reveal animations: `opacity 0→1` + `translateY(24px→0)`, `600ms`, cubic-bezier `.16 1 .3 1` (spring-like but restrained).
- Stagger delay per child: ~90ms increments.
- Transitions on interactive elements: `200ms ease`.
- No bounce, no overshoot on data or deal numbers — confident, settled.

### Hover & Interactive States

- CTA buttons: slight darkening (not opacity). Gradient shifts subtly on hover.
- Cards: `box-shadow` elevation increase on hover.
- Links: color shift to `--color-neon-red` on hover.

### Borders & Radius

- Cards and containers: `12px` radius standard, `20px` for prominent cards, `9999px` for pill buttons/tags.
- No decorative borders as brand device (no left-border-only accent cards).
- Thin `rgba(0,0,0,0.10)` dividers where needed.

### Shadows

- Minimal shadow use — elevate only when differentiating z-layers.
- Values: `0 1px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.08)` (sm), `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.10)` (md), `0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.13)` (lg).

### Layout & Spacing

- 120px page margin on slides and large canvases.
- 4px base grid. Most spacers in multiples of 4 or 8.
- Safe areas strictly respected per format (see §6 format specs).

### Corner Radius

Buttons: pill (`9999px`). Cards: `12px`. Modal/overlay: `20px`. Tags/chips: `9999px`.

### Imagery Color Vibe

Warm — neon orange/red/pink tones. Natural daylight. Not cold, not desaturated, not monochrome. Commercial premium without luxury pretension.

## ICONOGRAPHY

Rappi does not define a proprietary icon system in the Brand Book. Key observations:

- **No icon font or SVG sprite provided** in the brand book assets.
- **Emoji: prohibited** in all campaign and UX writing contexts (see Content Fundamentals).
- **Unicode symbols**: not documented as a brand device.
- Components in this DS use **Lucide Icons** (via CDN) as a stand-in for icons in UI kit prototypes — this is a substitution and should be replaced with the actual Rappi icon system from Figma once available.
- The **bigote symbol** (`assets/logo/rappi-bigote.png`) is the brand's graphic device / symbol. It can be used as a decorative/supporting element in compositions where the full logo is present, or as primary mark at very small sizes (favicon, app icon).

**Intentional addition:** Lucide Icons (CDN) used in UI kit components as a placeholder icon set. Replace with official Rappi icons from the Figma Design System file.
